# Changelog

All released changes are recorded here. Versions follow semantic versioning; beta releases remain prereleases while native platform checks are incomplete.

## 1.0.0-beta.1 — 2026-10-20

First complete public source and portable beta release.

- Visualize team capacity and feature forecasts with individual working-day estimates, allocations, calendars, dependencies and competing work.
- Inspect a feature from its name in the Features list: dates, person/team estimates, scheduled effort and the constraints shaping its forecast.
- Choose Ignore leave per feature to keep personal leave from changing that feature's forecast. Holidays, weekends and competing bookings continue to apply.
- Try changes in What-if planning, compare dates, and apply or discard the experiment.
- Explore a fictional sample workspace without changing saved data; use optional sprint planning, accepted commitments, timeline PDFs and private backups.
- Ship bundled local runtimes for Apple silicon Mac, Intel Mac and Windows x64 with dependency notices and SHA-256 checksums.

The beta is unsigned and Mac packages are not notarized. Apple silicon package lifecycle is tested locally. Native Intel Mac, Windows 10/11 and fresh-download security-prompt checks remain pending. Forecasts depend on the inputs entered; saved data stays on the user's computer.
